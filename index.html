// Pipeline

How It Works

Six autonomous steps from raw filing to actionable intelligence.

01
EDGAR Ingestion
Fetches live 10-K filings for any ticker via edgartools. Handles XBRL, filing indexing, and multi-year retrieval automatically.
02
Item 1A Extraction
Surgically isolates the Risk Factors section. This is where companies are legally required to disclose every known risk — raw signal source.
03
Signal Quantification
Converts prose to 4 numerical signals using keyword frequency, context weighting, and normalized scoring across fiscal years.
04
Regime Shift Detection
Computes YoY deltas and percentage changes. Flags structural breaks — not just noise — using threshold-based regime detection.
05
Market Correlation
Maps risk levels to 6-month forward returns to test whether elevated risk signals are priced in at time of filing.
06
AI Interpretation
GPT-4o reads the risk shifts and generates human-readable explanations of what changed, why it changed, and what it might mean for the stock.
