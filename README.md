## Overview
Titan is an AI-powered forensic analytics platform that analyzes SEC 10-K filings to detect **risk evolution**, **risk regime shifts**, and **market reaction patterns** across multiple years.

Unlike traditional sentiment tools, Titan focuses on **structural risk intelligence** — enabling investors, analysts, and researchers to understand *how and why* corporate risk changes over time.

---

## Key Capabilities
- 📄 **Automated SEC Filing Ingestion (EDGAR)**
- 📊 **Quantified Risk Signals (Uncertainty, Regulatory, Litigation, Supply Chain)**
- 📈 **Multi-Year Risk Shift Detection (YoY deltas & % change)**
- 📉 **Risk vs Forward 6-Month Market Returns**
- 🤖 **Autonomous AI Risk Interpretation**
- 🧠 **Latest Dominant Risk Intelligence**

---

## Tech Stack
- **Backend:** Python, pandas
- **SEC Data:** edgartools (EDGAR + XBRL)
- **AI Layer:** OpenAI (LLM-based interpretation)
- **Frontend:** Streamlit (custom dark UI)
- **Visualization:** Native Streamlit charts

---

## How It Works
1. Fetches live SEC filings and XBRL financials
2. Extracts Item 1A Risk Factors
3. Converts textual risk into quantitative signals
4. Builds multi-year risk time series
5. Computes risk shifts and market response
6. Generates AI-driven explanations
